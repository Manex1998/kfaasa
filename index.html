    Who are we?

    Kidney Failure Awareness and Advocacy SA (KFAASA) is a non-profit organisation dedicated to raising awareness and driving advocacy for Kidney for kidney health and the prevention of kidney failure.
    We exist to educate the public, influence health policy, and amplify the voices of those affected by kidney disease across South Africa.
    Our mission is to ensure that kidney health becomes a national priority through informed communities, responsive health systems, and strong advocacy.
    At KFAASA, We believe that awareness saves lives.

    Raising Awareness, Saving Lives

    At Kidney Awareness and Advocacy South Africa(KFAASA), we believe that knowledge is the first step towards prevention. Through our awareness and advocacy efforts, we aim to educate South Africans about kidney health, risk factors, and the importance of early detenction. Many people only discover kidney disease at an advanced stage, when treatment options are limited - button with the right information, kidney failure can often be prevented or managed earlier.
    Our awareness campaigns bring vital health education to communities, schools, and workplaces, ensuring that everyone understands how lifestyle choices, conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure, and regular screening play a role in kidney health. By working closely with healthcare professionals, public institutions, and corporate wellness programs, KFAASA transforms complex medical information into accessible.

    Community Engagement

    We collaborate with healthcare professionals, wellness programs, and public platforms to bring kidney health awareness directly to workplaces, schools, and communities.

    Advocacy & Policy Influence

    We engage with government departments, healthcare stakeholders, and corporate wellness programs to promote policies that prioritise kidney health and sustainable dialysis care.
